## 02 — Who We Are
Vietnam Clothing helps business buyers turn product briefs and technical specifications into finished apparel and merchandise. We coordinate a network of specialist manufacturers and suppliers in Vietnam, while managing product development, sampling, cost planning, production follow-up, inspections, packing and delivery.

We serve brands, distributors, procurement teams and merchandise sourcing companies that need a reliable local execution partner.

## 03 — What We Source
- Apparel: T-shirts, polos, hoodies, sweatshirts, jerseys, jackets, shorts and trousers.
- Merchandise & accessories: caps, hats, fabric bags, bandanas, scarves and selected promotional products.
- Decorations: screen printing, embroidery, heat transfer, garment wash and finishing — subject to specification and supplier feasibility.
- Materials: knitted and woven fabrics, labels, trims, customized packaging.

## 04 — Delivery Model
1. Review buyer specifications, target quantities, budget and market.
2. Evaluate sourcing routes, materials, MOQ, lead time and feasibility.
3. Develop samples and confirm technical specifications.
4. Agree quotation, purchase order, milestone plan and quality requirements.
5. Coordinate manufacturing, monitor progress and conduct inspections.
6. Pack, document and arrange delivery to agreed Incoterms where available.

## 05 — Product Development & Quality
Our coordination scope may include pattern/sample development, fabric sourcing, print/embroidery approvals, inline quality checks, final inspection and packing-list control.
Technical feasibility, certification and test requirements are agreed case by case. Do not claim certifications without verifiable documents.

## 06 — How We Work with Buyers
- A single accountable project contact
- Clear sampling and approval checkpoints
- MOQ-based pricing with transparent scope
- Production plans matched to complexity and supplier capacity
- Early risk escalation and documented change requests
- Order-level cost and delivery tracking

Typical planning reference for complex multi-style merchandise: ~2 weeks product development plus 30–40 days bulk production after approvals, plus QC and transport. These are planning assumptions, not guaranteed lead times.
