# ATTD Blank Supply B2B — Business & UX Architecture

## Positioning
ATTD Blank Supply is the B2B source for standardized blank apparel that buyers can print, resell, or reuse across recurring projects.

## Primary buyer segments
1. Print / embroidery shops
2. Uniform dealers and resellers
3. Agencies and event producers
4. Local brands and companies buying blanks

## Top-level site entry model
- **Dự án doanh nghiệp** — uniforms, gifts, campaign projects
- **Nguồn áo trơn B2B** — standardized blank supply, stock/reorder oriented
- **OEM / Merchandise** — custom product development and multi-SKU programs

## Blank Supply funnel
1. Choose product family: T-shirt / Polo / Oversize / Hoodie-Sweatshirt / future blank accessories
2. Compare commercial attributes: form, fabric, GSM, colors, sizes
3. Understand supply: MOQ, price-from, availability / lead time
4. Choose action:
   - Xem giá sỉ
   - Kiểm tra tồn kho
   - Lấy mẫu
5. Optional add-ons after blank selection:
   - Printing
   - Embroidery
   - Packaging
   - OEM modifications

## Navigation principle
Blank Supply is a business destination, not one item inside a long services menu.

SEO pages may target different search intents, but they must converge into the same commercial product/funnel model.

## Product card requirements
Every blank-product card should prioritize:
- Product name / SKU
- Form
- Fabric + GSM
- Price-from or "Nhận giá sỉ" state
- MOQ
- Color count / key colors
- Size range
- Supply status / lead time where reliable
- Primary CTA: **Xem giá sỉ**

Secondary entry: **Xem chi tiết**.

## PDP requirements
Above the fold should answer:
- Đây là áo gì?
- Form/chất liệu/GSM?
- Có màu/size nào?
- MOQ bao nhiêu?
- Có sẵn hay cần sản xuất?
- Giá B2B bắt đầu thế nào?
- Có phù hợp in/thêu không?
- Tôi cần làm gì tiếp theo?

Primary CTA stack:
1. **Xem giá sỉ**
2. **Kiểm tra tồn kho**
3. **Lấy mẫu**

## Conversion rules
- Do not force project-service language onto blank buyers.
- Do not lead with discount percentage as the core value proposition.
- Lead with repeatability: stable SKU, consistent quality, predictable supply, easy reorder.
- Printing / embroidery / OEM are contextual upsells, not the first decision.

## Homepage entry
Homepage must make Blank Supply visible as one of the core commercial paths:
> Cần nguồn áo trơn để in, bán lại hoặc triển khai dự án?
> Chọn form, chất liệu, màu và nhận giá B2B theo số lượng.

CTA: **Xem nguồn áo trơn**

## Phase 1 — Public conversion layer
- IA + navigation entry
- Homepage Blank Supply entry
- Blank Supply landing hub
- Product cards normalization
- PDP commercial information hierarchy
- Intent-specific CTAs
- Desktop/mobile conversion QA

## Phase 2 — Repeat-revenue layer
- B2B/dealer account
- Tiered pricing
- Stock availability
- Saved products
- One-click / rapid reorder
- Quote-to-CRM linkage
- Purchase history and recurring recommendations

## Success criteria
- A buyer reaches a relevant blank product in 2–3 steps.
- A buyer can understand MOQ, material, form, and commercial next action without contacting ATTD first.
- A repeat buyer can identify and reorder the same SKU with minimal friction.
- SEO landing pages feed one consistent buying system rather than creating parallel product experiences.
